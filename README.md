## What I'm building

### 🎮 HEL — extraction-survival melee, Unreal Engine 5
Built on **ACF** (Ascent Combat Framework) with **AGLS** locomotion, **MetaHuman** characters and **Gaea** terrain. Hero module, flight and jump traversal, combat tuning. 253 GB of project with its own documented knowledge base.

### 🍽️ Alvor Reservas — reservation SaaS with AI
For **two restaurants in Alvor, Portugal**, thirty years in business, still taking bookings by phone and Messenger.

One constraint shaped everything: *the restaurants can't stop*. So nothing gets replaced. Phone and Messenger keep working, and every channel lands in one panel through a `channel` field. The team adopts it in stages, and anything can be undone.

`Next.js` · `Supabase` with row-level security · an AI layer that's provider-agnostic, so testing costs nothing and production runs the best model by changing one variable.

### 🐎 Pantanal — RedM server with a working economy
Farms, animal genetics and breeding, cooperatives, a mint, workshops, industries, exports. Dozens of Lua resources plus a **Next.js companion app** with dashboards and player guides.

### 💳 SMM Panel — payments and reseller integration
**MercadoPago** checkout, Discord auth. The webhook checks payment status at the source instead of trusting the callback, refuses to credit twice, and moves status and balance together or not at all.

## How I work

**Decisions get written down.** Every project carries a `DECISIONS.md` — context, decision, reasoning. Six months later I know *why*, not just *what*. It's less about discipline than about not trusting my own memory.

**Constraints before features.** Alvor started from "the restaurant can't stop", not from a feature list. The constraint wrote most of the architecture on its own.

**Cost is an architecture problem.** Free models while building, the best one in production, one environment variable apart.
